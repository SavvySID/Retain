BEFORE YOU UPLOAD

A clean file, a reliable result.

  1. Start with the sample.Use its exact column headers. Customer ID is optional; the predictor columns are required.
  2. Keep values consistent.Use the supported service categories. A customer without internet cannot have internet add-ons.
  3. Review your predictions.The output contains probability, predicted churn and risk group. It does not include causal explanations or verified outcomes.

Know the limits.
This is a fictional-company research benchmark, not a production forecast. Churn is the source dataset’s last-month label. SHAP describes model associations, not causal effects. Suggested actions have no measured uplift. Excluding gender and age indicators does not establish fairness. New customer populations require fresh validation.
